SOP creator

A Claude tool I built that turns existing company documents into a draft standard operating procedure. It searches SharePoint, lets you pick the source files, and writes the SOP strictly from what those files say.

My role: Built the SOP creator in Claude using the Microsoft 365 connector.

Outcome: Sent to a client's CFO and HR manager to create standard operating procedures from their SharePoint site, along with a Markdown (.md) file of their branding so every SOP comes out branded.

How it works

1Search SharePoint by keyword and file type across connected sites.
2Pick the documents that describe the process: checklists, emails, old procedures.
3The tool reads each file through the Microsoft 365 connector.
4Claude drafts the SOP: purpose, scope, roles, numbered steps and exceptions.

SOP: New Vendor Setup

Drafted from the selected source documents. Every step below is supported by at least one source.

Purpose

Make sure every new vendor is verified, insured and set up correctly before the first purchase order is issued.

Scope

Applies to all new material suppliers and subcontractors. Does not cover one-time credit card purchases under $500.

Roles and responsibilities

RoleResponsibility
RequesterSubmits the vendor request form with the vendor's contact details and reason for use.
AP SpecialistCollects the W-9 and certificate of insurance, and enters the vendor in the accounting system.
ControllerApproves vendors with expected annual spend over $25,000.

Procedure

  1. Requester submits the vendor request form.
  2. AP Specialist emails the vendor for a signed W-9 and a current certificate of insurance.
  3. AP Specialist confirms the insurance meets the minimum limits and lists the company as additional insured.
  4. If expected annual spend is over $25,000, the Controller reviews and approves the vendor.
  5. AP Specialist creates the vendor record and attaches the W-9 and insurance certificate.
  6. AP Specialist sets an insurance expiration reminder 30 days before the policy end date.
  7. AP Specialist notifies the requester that the vendor is active and POs can be issued.

Notes and exceptions

  • No purchase orders may be issued to a vendor without a W-9 on file.
  • Expired insurance puts the vendor on hold until a new certificate is received.

What makes it reliable

  • Grounded in sources. The prompt tells Claude to use only what the selected documents support and to keep the SOP short when the sources are thin, rather than invent steps.
  • Live company data. It reads real files through the Microsoft 365 connector, so the draft reflects how the business actually works today.
  • Consistent structure. Every SOP comes out with the same sections, which makes a whole library easy to read and maintain.
  • Human in the loop. The person chooses the sources and reviews the draft before it becomes the official procedure.

Built with Claude and the Microsoft 365 connector. Company, people and document names on this page are fictional.
