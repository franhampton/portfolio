How we'll work together

  • Weekly check-in: 30 minutes with the client sponsor, same day and time each week.
  • Weekly status update (D7): sent after each check-in, listing progress, next steps, open requests and risks.
  • Requests for information: tracked in one list; the client replies within the agreed timeframe or flags a blocker.
  • Interviews: 50 minutes each, one person per session, scheduled through the sponsor.
  • Mapping validation: 30 minute follow-up sessions where process owners confirm their map.
  • Communication: email for requests and documents; a shared folder for files.
  • Changes to scope: in writing, approved by both sides before work starts.

What we need from the client

These items let interviews start on schedule; the first five are needed before the on-site week.

  • Name the project sponsor and a systems lead
  • Org chart and staff roster with roles
  • Confirm the interview list and hold calendar time
  • Complete the digital operations survey (sent after kickoff)
  • Read-only access or screen-share walkthroughs for the case management system, the donor database and the accounting system
  • Software list with subscription and licensing details
  • Sample documents: volunteer application, enrollment forms, match support check-in notes, a board report, a gift batch
  • Current reports, dashboards and spreadsheets in regular use, including any waitlist tracker
  • Approved logo files and brand guidelines for deliverables
