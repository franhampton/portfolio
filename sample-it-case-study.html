IT leadership case study

Building an IT department from the ground up

Over 11 years as IT Manager at Mid-South Roof Systems, a $90M+ roofing contractor with 300 employees across two locations, I founded the IT department and took it from on-premises servers and paper to the cloud, strong security and remote-ready operations.

My role: IT Manager at Mid-South Roof Systems. I founded and led the IT department.

23% → 82%security score in one month
300employees moved to remote work in 2020
27,000+support tickets, 98.8% satisfaction
80%less paper with digital workflows

The starting point

The company had no IT department. I founded it and grew it to support 300 users and 580+ Windows and Apple devices across two locations, starting from infrastructure that ran on premises.

Security: 23% to 82% in one month

I raised the Entra (Azure) security score from 23% to 82% in a single month by turning on multi-factor authentication, blocking legacy authentication, and deploying SentinelOne, Huntress and BitLocker. I also created and tested disaster recovery and business continuity plans, deployed OneDrive backup for 130 accounts, and chaired the committee that added keycard access and CCTV.

Moving to the cloud

  • Migrated 120 AD accounts to Entra ID (Azure AD) and 130 users to Office 365
  • Moved file shares to a 2 TB SharePoint site
  • Rolled out Smartsheet and Microsoft Teams, and a VoIP upgrade that included a Webex migration integrated with Teams

That groundwork paid off in 2020, when I moved all 300 employees to remote work at the start of the pandemic.

Running IT like a service

Support

Resolved 27,000+ tickets through Atera with a 98.8% satisfaction rate, using AnyDesk for remote support.

Process

Wrote 10 IT standard operating procedures and policies, from ticketing and cybersecurity to onboarding and offboarding.

Training

Built SharePoint sites and a 4,000+ video training library on Outlook, Excel, Word, Dataforma and Terial.

Projects

Introduced a "Project Champion" system for ownership across departments on time and attendance, Power BI and collaboration projects.

Process mapping

Interviewed staff and held review sessions to map processes for 10 departments in Visio and Lucidchart, informing software decisions.

Vendors

Negotiated and managed agreements with 60+ vendors and upgraded 200+ devices.

What carried into my consulting work

  • Security and continuity first, because everything else depends on them.
  • Process before software. Mapping how departments really worked kept technology decisions grounded.
  • Adoption is the job. Training libraries, plain-language updates to leadership and project champions made the changes stick.

Results from my time as IT Manager at Mid-South Roof Systems, 2012 to 2023.
